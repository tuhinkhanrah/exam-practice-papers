  <name><text>[ch010-unit-10-ecology-and-environment] NEET_UG_BIOLOGY_MCQ_Q02 - Intrinsic rate of natural increase calculation</text></name>
  <questiontext format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p>In a freshwater pond, a population of \(800\) <em>Hydrilla</em> individuals experiences \(120\) new individuals added through natality and \(40\) individuals lost through mortality over a period of one year, while immigration equals emigration. Assuming unlimited resources and constant per capita birth and death rates, what are the intrinsic rate of natural increase (\(r\)) and the net population size (\(N\)) at the end of that year?</p>]]></text></questiontext>
  <generalfeedback format="html"><text><![CDATA[<p><strong>Step 1: Given Data and Conditions</strong><br/>Initial population size, \(N_0 = 800\).<br/>Number of births in one year, \(B = 120\).<br/>Number of deaths in one year, \(D = 40\).<br/>Time interval, \(\Delta t = 1\text{ year}\).<br/>Immigration equals emigration (\(I = E\)), so net migration is zero.</p><p><strong>Step 2: Assumptions and Sign Conventions</strong><br/>Natality contributes positively to population growth, whereas mortality contributes negatively. Under unlimited resources, growth follows the exponential model with constant per capita birth rate \(b\) and per capita death rate \(d\).</p><p><strong>Step 3: Relevant Formulae and Principles</strong><br/>Per capita birth rate: \(b = \frac{B}{N_0}\)<br/>Per capita death rate: \(d = \frac{D}{N_0}\)<br/>Intrinsic rate of natural increase: \(r = b - d\)<br/>Net change in population over one year: \(\Delta N = r N_0 = B - D\)<br/>Final population size: \(N = N_0 + \Delta N\)</p><p><strong>Step 4: Step-by-Step Working</strong><br/>Calculate the per capita birth rate:<br/>\[b = \frac{120}{800} = 0.15\text{ year}^{-1}\]<br/>Calculate the per capita death rate:<br/>\[d = \frac{40}{800} = 0.05\text{ year}^{-1}\]<br/>Calculate the intrinsic rate of natural increase:<br/>\[r = b - d = 0.15 - 0.05 = 0.10\text{ year}^{-1}\]<br/>Calculate the net increase in population size:<br/>\[\Delta N = r N_0 = 0.10 \times 800 = 80\]<br/>Calculate the population size at the end of one year:<br/>\[N = 800 + 80 = 880\]</p><p><strong>Step 5: Check and Final Answer</strong><br/>Checking via direct count balance: \(N = 800 + 120 - 40 = 880\), which matches the rate-based calculation, and \(r = 0.10\text{ year}^{-1}\) is positive as births exceed deaths. Therefore, \(r = 0.10\text{ year}^{-1}\) and \(N = 880\).</p>]]></text></generalfeedback>
